A long-only cbBTC mean-reversion bot. It waits for the price to stretch 2.5 standard deviations under its 4-hour mean, buys half on the first up-bar, adds the other half if the dip deepens past 3.25, and sells the whole position on the bounce (z above 1.5), on a 2% stop under cost, or after 8 hours.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts < batches.ndjson
```
